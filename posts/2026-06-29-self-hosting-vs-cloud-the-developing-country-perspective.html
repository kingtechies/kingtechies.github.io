# Self-Hosting vs Cloud: The Developing Country Perspective AWS: $5,000/month. My VPS: $40/month. Here's why self-hosting matters in developing countries. ## The Forex Problem Cloud bills in dollars. Revenue in naira. When naira devalued 70%, my AWS bill tripled. Self-hosting eliminates forex exposure. ## The Bandwidth Reality Cloud charges for data transfer. Aggressive caching, offline-first, compression — every kilobyte saved is money saved. ## The Latency Problem Closest AWS to Lagos: 200ms+. Self-hosted Lagos VPS: 5ms. ## My Stack Docker + Nginx + PostgreSQL + Redis. $40/month. 50K users. 99.9% uptime. The developing world doesn't need cloud.
